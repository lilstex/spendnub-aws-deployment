# 🌀 ShotNub-SpendNub Preview

### _Financial Engineering for the Modern Developer_

**ShotNub-SpendNub** isn't just another expense tracker—it’s a rule-based financial engine. Built on the **50/30/20 principle** (and fully customizable beyond it), SpendNub automatically splits your income into dedicated "Category Pulses" or sinking funds.

By utilizing a **Double-Entry Ledger Architecture**, every dollar is accounted for, providing an immutable audit trail of your financial health.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Smart Income Splitting:** Log a single income source and watch the engine automatically distribute funds across your pre-defined budget buckets.
- **The "Sinking Fund" Engine:** Unlike traditional apps that reset monthly, SpendNub tracks a **Rolling Balance**. Unspent money in your "Wants" or "Utility" buckets carries over, building your net worth in real-time.
- **Atomic Ledger Updates:** Every transaction generates a unique Ledger ID. Deleting an income or expense automatically "reverses" the ledger entry, ensuring your balance is never out of sync.
- **Categorical Analytics:** Deep-dive into specific buckets to see your "Total Available" vs. "Monthly Allocation."
- **Developer-Centric Stack:** Built with performance and type-safety in mind using NestJS and Next.js.
